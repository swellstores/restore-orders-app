Bring back orders that were deleted by mistake. Pick a period, see the orders deleted in it, and restore the ones you need with their original number and data.

- **Find deleted orders.** Search any period and list its deleted orders with their number, dates, total, customer account and name. Orders that already exist again aren't listed, and an order deleted more than once shows only its latest deletion.
- **Leave out canceled orders.** Only not canceled is on by default and skips orders that were canceled when they were deleted. Turn it off to list them too, with a Canceled badge.
- **Check before you restore.** Sort by deletion or creation date, and click an order to see the full data it had when it was deleted.
- **Restore in one click.** Select orders on a page or all results and click Restore. The page shows how many were restored, says why any weren't, and refreshes the list.

No setup needed. Install the app and open Orders > Restore orders.
