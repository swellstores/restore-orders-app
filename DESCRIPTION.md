Bring back orders that were deleted by mistake. Pick a period, see the orders deleted in it, and restore the ones you need with their original number.

The app reads the deletion events Swell already records, so it also finds orders deleted before you installed it. Restoring sends no emails, calls no webhooks and doesn't change your stock.

- **Find deleted orders.** Search any period and list its deleted orders with their number, dates, total, customer account and name. Orders that exist again aren't listed, and an order deleted more than once shows only its latest deletion.
- **Leave out canceled orders.** Only not canceled is on by default and skips orders that were canceled when they were deleted. Turn it off to list them too, with a Canceled badge.
- **Check before you restore.** Sort by date deleted or date created, and click an order to see the full data it had when it was deleted.
- **Restore what you choose.** Select orders on a page or all results, click Restore and confirm. The page shows how many were restored, says why any weren't, and refreshes the list. Only staff who can manage orders can restore; staff who can only view orders can search.
- **Original number.** A restored order keeps its number, dates, status, items and totals. Its payments, shipments and returns weren't deleted with it, so they show on it again.

Setup takes a minute. Install the app in each environment you need and open Orders > Restore orders. There's nothing to configure.
